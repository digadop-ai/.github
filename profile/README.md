# Digadop

<img src="digadop-logo.svg" alt="Digadop" width="220" />

Stony Point, Inc. (d/b/a Digadop) builds the Digadop family of AI-powered software for
Salesforce teams, and also trains Salesforce teams through Stony Point.

## Products

- **[Who Sees What](https://whoseeswhat.com/)** (live): audits Salesforce permissions and
  exposure risk, so security and admin teams can see who has access to sensitive data, and why.
- **[Digadop Help](https://digadop.ai/products/digadop-help/)** (live): delivers guidance and
  documentation inside Salesforce, on the page each user is already on.
- **[Messigent](https://messigent.com/)** (early access): one to one lifecycle email and lead
  capture with deliverability built in; Salesforce is an optional connector, not a requirement.
- **[Clionyx](https://clionyx.com/)** (beta): role specific Salesforce documentation, designed
  to be generated by AI from your org's real configuration.
- **[Lynceon](https://lynceon.ai/)** (coming soon): turns Salesforce access into security
  judgment, with ranked risk findings and remediation guidance.
- **[Chronovix](https://chronovix.com/)** (coming soon): backup for your Salesforce org, with
  selective point in time restore.

Learn more at [digadop.ai](https://digadop.ai/), or about the company behind it at
[stonyp.com](https://www.stonyp.com/).
